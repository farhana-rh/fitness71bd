The gym

Facilities
& services

Six thousand square feet of working equipment, and eight training programmes built around what members in Dhaka actually ask us for.

Facilities

Twelve areas,
one training floor

Laid out across two levels so strength work, cardio and conditioning are not competing for the same square metre at 6 pm.

01

Strength Training

Six power racks with safety pins, competition bars, calibrated bumper plates and two pulling platforms.

02

Free Weights

A full dumbbell run from 2.5 kg to 50 kg, flat, incline and decline benches, EZ bars and fixed barbells.

03

Cardio Zone

Treadmills, upright and recumbent bikes, rowers, a ski erg and stair climbers — all with their own screens.

04

Functional Training

A 15-metre turf lane for sled pushes and carries, plus battle ropes, plyo boxes and medicine balls.

05

Kettlebell & Conditioning

Kettlebells from 8 kg to 40 kg, slam balls and an open mat area for circuits and mobility work.

06

Olympic Platform

A dedicated lifting platform with bumper plates and a competition bar for cleans, snatches and jerks.

07

Machine Circuit

Plate-loaded and selectorised machines covering every major movement, laid out as a workable circuit.

08

Cable & Rig Area

Dual adjustable cables, a functional trainer, pull-up stations and rings for assisted and bodyweight work.

09

Personal Training Area

A quieter corner of the upper floor kept free of general traffic so coached sessions are not interrupted.

10

Stretch & Recovery

Matted stretching space with foam rollers, bands and mobility tools away from the main walkways.

11

Changing & Lockers

Separate men's and ladies' changing rooms with day lockers, hot showers, and filtered drinking water.

12

Reception & Front Desk

Staffed from opening to close for check-ins, membership queries, day passes and trainer bookings.

Services

Training programmes

Every programme starts with an assessment and a conversation. We will tell you honestly which of these you need — and which you do not.

Personal Training

One-to-one coaching with a trainer who writes your programme, watches every set and keeps a written record of your lifts. Sold in blocks of 8, 12 or 24 sessions.

8, 12 or 24 sessions

Weight Loss Programmes

A training and nutrition plan built around food you actually eat — rice, dal, fish, roti — with fortnightly check-ins and measurements. No shakes required.

8–16 week blocks

Muscle Building

Structured hypertrophy blocks with tracked sets, progressive overload and planned deloads, so you know exactly what you are lifting next month and why.

Programmed in blocks

Strength Training

Squat, bench, deadlift and overhead press coached from the ground up. Suitable whether you are learning the lifts or chasing a number you have been stuck at for a year.

Barbell-led

Fitness Assessment

A 45-minute session covering body composition, resting heart rate, basic movement screening and a conversation about what you actually want out of training.

45 minutes

Beginner Training

Three guided sessions included with every membership. How the equipment works, how to warm up, how to fail a lift safely, and a first programme you can run on your own.

Included free

Custom Workout Programmes

A written four to twelve week plan you take away and follow yourself, reviewed and adjusted at the end of each block. For members who train alone but want direction.

Written plan

Group Fitness

Small-group conditioning, circuit and mobility classes through the week. Numbers capped at twelve so the coach can still correct everyone in the room.

Max 12 per class
