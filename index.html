Who we are

A gym that takes
training seriously

The Fitness 71 started with a simple frustration: too many gyms in Dhaka sell memberships and forget about you afterwards.

We built the opposite. A floor with enough weight to keep you progressing for years, coaches who know your name and your programme, and a standard of cleanliness and maintenance we do not compromise on. Whether you have trained for a decade or have never touched a barbell, you get the same attention.

The name is a nod to 1971 — to a generation that refused to settle. We think that is the right mindset for a gym.
