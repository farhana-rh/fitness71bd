How we coach

What a session
with us looks like

The same four things happen whether you are on a personal training package or just doing your own thing on the floor.

01

We ask first

What you have done before, what hurts, how many days a week you can realistically train. Then we build from there.

02

We write it down

Your programme and every session's numbers are recorded. Progress you cannot see is progress you will not believe in.

03

We correct as we go

Technique gets fixed in the set, not in a lecture afterwards. Expect to be interrupted mid-rep — that is the job.

04

We adjust monthly

Plans get reviewed every four weeks. If something is not working we change it rather than telling you to try harder.
