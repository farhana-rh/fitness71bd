Privacy policy

How The Fitness 71 collects, uses and protects the personal information you give us.

Template wording — have this reviewed by a lawyer before you publish

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what we do with your information when you enquire about membership, join the gym, or use this website. It applies to The Fitness 71, House 42, Road 11, Banani, Dhaka 1213, Bangladesh.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the gym and answer your enquiries:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number and email address when you submit an enquiry form or sign up at reception.
  • Membership records — your plan, start and renewal dates, payment history and attendance.
  • Health and fitness information — only what you choose to share during an assessment or with a trainer, such as injuries, medical conditions relevant to exercise, and body measurements.
  • Website data — basic, anonymous usage statistics if analytics are enabled, such as which pages are visited and on what kind of device.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a trial session or membership.
  • To manage your membership, process payments and send renewal reminders.
  • To write and adjust your training programme safely.
  • To let you know about changes to opening hours, class times or facilities.

We will only send you promotional messages if you have agreed to receive them, and every message will include a way to stop them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. It may be handled by:

  • Our own staff and trainers, only as far as their job requires.
  • Payment providers and banks, to process membership payments.
  • Service providers who host this website or our membership software.
  • Government bodies or law enforcement, where we are legally required to do so.

Health information

Anything you tell a trainer about your health is treated as confidential and shared only with coaches who need it to train you safely. You are free to withhold it, though it may limit what we can responsibly programme for you.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for up to 12 months if you do not join. Membership and payment records are kept for as long as you are a member and for a reasonable period afterwards, to meet accounting and legal obligations.

Keeping it secure

Paper records are kept locked at reception. Digital records are password protected and access is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will tell you promptly if something goes wrong.

Cookies

This website uses only what is needed to function. If we add analytics or embedded content — such as a Google Map or a social media feed — those services may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Stop sending you promotional messages.

Email info@thefitness71.com or speak to reception and we will deal with it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be posted at reception as well.
