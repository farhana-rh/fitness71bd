The rules of membership at The Fitness 71, and what you can expect from us in return.

These terms apply to every membership, day pass and personal training package sold by The Fitness 71, House 42, Road 11, Banani, Dhaka 1213.

1. Membership

  • Membership is personal and cannot be transferred or shared. Cards may be checked at reception.
  • Members must be 16 or over. Members aged 16 and 17 need written consent from a parent or guardian and must complete an induction before training unsupervised.
  • Your membership starts on the date shown on your receipt and runs for the term you paid for.
  • We do not renew memberships automatically. You choose whether to renew when your term ends.

2. Payment

  • All fees are payable in advance in Bangladeshi taka (৳).
  • A one-time registration fee applies to new members, and is waived on 6-month and annual plans.
  • Fees are not refundable once a term has started, except where required by law or where we are unable to provide the service.
  • We may change our prices, but never during a term you have already paid for. Existing members get at least 30 days' notice.

3. Freezing and cancellation

  • Memberships of 3 months or longer may be frozen once per term for up to 30 days, at no charge, with written notice to reception.
  • You may cancel at any time. There is no cancellation fee, but unused time on a term is not refunded unless the cancellation is for documented medical reasons.
  • We may cancel a membership without refund for serious or repeated breaches of the gym rules.

4. Health and safety

  • You confirm that you are in good health and medically fit to exercise. If you have a condition that affects your training, tell a trainer before you start.
  • You train at your own risk. Use equipment as instructed, and ask a coach if you are not sure.
  • Our staff are not medical professionals. Nothing they advise is a substitute for medical care.
  • Report any injury, accident or faulty equipment to reception immediately.

5. Gym rules

  • Rerack your weights and wipe down equipment after use.
  • Clean, closed training shoes must be worn on the floor at all times.
  • Do not drop bumper plates outside the designated platforms.
  • Share equipment during busy periods, and limit cardio machines to 30 minutes when people are waiting.
  • Photography and filming are allowed only with the consent of anyone in the frame, and never in changing rooms.
  • Abusive, intimidating or discriminatory behaviour results in immediate cancellation without refund.

6. Personal training

  • Personal training sessions are valid for three months from purchase.
  • Sessions cancelled with less than 12 hours' notice are treated as used.
  • If a coach cancels, the session is rescheduled at no cost to you.
  • Packages are non-transferable between members.

7. Property and lockers

  • Lockers are for use during your session only and must be emptied before you leave.
  • We are not responsible for loss of or damage to personal property brought into the gym. Do not leave valuables unattended.
  • Items left in lockers overnight may be removed and held at reception for 14 days.

8. Opening hours and access

Published opening hours may change on public holidays, during maintenance, or for reasons outside our control. We will give as much notice as we reasonably can at reception and on our social media channels. Short closures do not entitle you to a refund.

9. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for anything else that cannot be limited under the law of Bangladesh. Otherwise our liability to you is limited to the fees you have paid for the current membership term.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Bangladesh, and the courts of Dhaka have jurisdiction over any dispute.

11. Contact

Questions about these terms? Speak to reception or email info@thefitness71.com.
