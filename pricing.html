Questions

Before you join

If the answer you need is not here, call the gym — someone picks up.

What are the gym hours?

We open Saturday to Thursday from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Friday from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Ladies-only hours run 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, Saturday to Thursday, with a female coach on duty for the whole slot.

We close on two public holidays a year and post the dates at reception and on our Facebook page well in advance.

Is personal training available?

Yes. Personal training is included in the Premium membership, and any member on Basic or Standard can buy a package of 8, 12 or 24 sessions separately.

You can also book a single session first if you would rather try a coach before committing to a block.

Do you offer trial sessions?

Your first session is free, no card required. Come in during opening hours, bring shoes you can train in, and someone will show you round and take you through a workout.

We also sell day passes if you are visiting Dhaka or want a few sessions before joining properly.

What equipment is available?

Six power racks, two Olympic platforms, dumbbells from 2.5 kg to 50 kg, a full machine circuit, cables, kettlebells, a 15-metre turf lane with sleds, and a cardio deck with treadmills, bikes, rowers and a ski erg.

The full list is on the facilities page, and reception can tell you about anything specific.

Is there a joining fee?

There is a one-time registration fee that covers your assessment, induction sessions and membership card. It is waived on 6-month and annual plans.

There are no other charges — no automatic renewal, no cancellation fee, and no charge to freeze your membership.

Are there student discounts?

Yes. Students with a valid ID from any university or college in Dhaka get a discount on all monthly and 3-month plans. Bring your ID to reception when you sign up.

We also run corporate rates for companies enrolling five or more staff — email us for details.

Can beginners join?

Most of our members were beginners when they joined. Every membership includes three guided induction sessions covering how the equipment works, how to warm up and a starting programme you can follow on your own.

Nobody here will make you feel out of place. If they do, tell us.
