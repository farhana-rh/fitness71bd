About us

Built in Dhaka,
for people who train

The Fitness 71 has been on Road 11 in Banani since 2019. This is who we are, why we opened, and what we are trying to do differently.

The gym

A training floor,
not a leisure club

We are a gym in the plain sense of the word. Weights, machines, cardio, coaches and a floor with enough room to use all of it properly.

About 6,000 square feet over two levels in Banani, ten minutes from Gulshan and Mohakhali. Six power racks, a full dumbbell run to 50 kg, a turf lane for sled and conditioning work, and a cardio deck that is never all occupied at once.

We capped our membership deliberately. A gym that sells 2,000 cards and fits 300 people is selling you a queue. We would rather grow slowly and keep the 6 pm rush usable.

Our story

It started because
we were annoyed

In 2018 the two of us were training partners paying for memberships at three different gyms in Dhaka between us. One had good bars but no air conditioning. One had air conditioning but four working dumbbells above 30 kg. The third kept a lovely reception area and a broken cable machine that stayed broken for eight months.

None of it was complicated to fix. It just needed someone who actually trained to be the one making the decisions.

We opened The Fitness 71 in a smaller unit on Road 11 in 2019 with a rack, a platform, a dumbbell run and a lot of borrowed money. We took the floor above us in 2022 when the waiting list got embarrassing. Most of what we have added since — the turf lane, the second squat area, the ladies-only hours — came from members asking for it.

The name is for 1971. Not as decoration: it is a reminder that the standard we hold ourselves to was set by people who had far more to lose than we do.

What drives us

Mission, vision
and the reason why

Our mission

To make properly coached strength training normal in Dhaka — affordable enough to keep, serious enough to work, and welcoming enough that a complete beginner walks in without dreading it.

Our vision

A city where every neighbourhood has a gym worth belonging to. We would rather be the gym other Dhaka gyms copy than the biggest chain with the thinnest service.

Why we exist

Because most people who quit the gym did not lack discipline — they lacked a plan, a working machine and someone who noticed they had stopped coming. We try to remove all three excuses.

The difference

What actually sets
this gym apart

Every gym claims these things. Here is specifically what we do about each one.

Equipment gets fixed in days, not months

We hold spares for the parts that fail most and log every fault on the floor. If something is out of service for more than a week, it is our failure and we will tell you when it is back.

Every member gets a starting plan

Three guided sessions are included with every membership, however cheap. You leave knowing how to warm up, which machines do what, and what your first twelve weeks look like.

Coaches are paid to coach, not to sell

Our trainers do not earn commission on memberships or supplements. If one of them tells you to buy something, it is because they think you need it.

Ladies-only hours with a female coach

2 to 5 pm, Saturday to Thursday, with Nusrat or Sadia on duty for the full slot. Not a curtain in the corner — the whole floor.

The power never goes out

Full generator backup covering lights, air conditioning and every machine. In Dhaka that is not a luxury, it is the difference between training and going home.

We will tell you if you do not need us

If a personal training package is not worth it for you yet, we will say so. Members who feel pushed into things do not renew, and we would rather keep you.
