Getting here

Find the gym

Road 11 runs between Kemal Ataturk Avenue and Road 27. We are on the left as you come from the Banani 11 junction, above the ground-floor shopfronts. Street parking is available after 6 PM; bike parking is inside the gate.

From Gulshan 2

Around 8 minutes by car outside peak hours via Road 11. CNG and ride-share drop-off is directly outside the gate.

From Mohakhali

About 10 minutes via Kemal Ataturk Avenue. Secure bicycle and motorbike parking inside the compound.

Quietest times

Before 9 AM and after 9:30 PM on weekdays. Saturday mornings and 6–8 PM are our busiest windows.
